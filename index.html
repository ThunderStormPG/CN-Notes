Cyber Security: Survival Guide

Unit 1 & Unit 2 - Hinglish Notes & Mnemonics

🛡️ Unit 1: Introduction to Cyber Security

1. Introduction to Information Systems

Ek Information System (IS) ek aisa system hai jo data ko collect, process, store aur distribute karta hai. Yeh sirf computer nahi hota, isme log aur process bhi shamil hote hain.

Components: Hardware, Software, Data, People, Process.

Mnemonic: Hum Sab Dost Process (Hardware, Software, Data, People, Process)

2. Types of Information Systems

  • TPS (Transaction Processing System): Daily basis ka kaam (e.g., ATM se paise nikalna, billing).
  • MIS (Management Information System): Managers ke liye reports banata hai (e.g., monthly sales report).
  • DSS (Decision Support System): Complex decisions lene me help karta hai (e.g., "Agar price badha dein toh kya hoga?").
  • EIS (Executive Information System): Top-level CEOs ke liye (overall company ka dashboard).
Mnemonic: Time Management Decision Executive (TPS -> MIS -> DSS -> EIS)

3. Information Security Concepts (CIA Triad)

Security ka pura base isi par tika hai:

  • Confidentiality (Secrecy): Data sirf authorized logo ko dikhe.
  • Integrity (Accuracy): Data ke sath koi chhed-chhad na ho.
  • Availability (Uplabdhata): Data jab chahiye tab available ho.
Mnemonic: CIA (Central Intelligence Agency) - Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability.

4. Computer, Network & Application Security

  • Computer Security: Aapke personal device (laptop/PC) ko bachana (Antivirus, Password).
  • Network Security: Data ko raste me bachana (Firewalls, Encryption, VPN).
  • Application Security: Software/App ko hack hone se bachana (Secure coding, OWASP guidelines).

5. Need for Information Security

  • Data loss/leak se bachne ke liye (Financial loss).
  • Company ki reputation bachane ke liye.
  • Privacy laws (GDPR) ka palan karne ke liye.
  • National security ke liye.

6. Information Assurance vs InfoSec

InfoSec ka matlab hai data ko attacks se bachana. Information Assurance (IA) thoda bada concept hai - iska matlab hai data secure hai aur uspe bharosa (trust) bhi hai. IA me Non-repudiation (koi ye na keh sake ki "maine ye message bheja hi nahi") shamil hota hai.

7. Security Risk Analysis

Risk ka matlab hai "Khatra". Formula yaad rakho:

Risk = Threat × Vulnerability × Impact

  • Threat: Kya attack ho sakta hai? (Hacker)
  • Vulnerability: Aapke system me kya weakness hai? (Weak password)
  • Impact: Agar attack hua toh kitna nuksan hoga?

🦠 Unit 2: Cyber Threats and Attacks

1. Cyber Threats & Threat Vectors

Cyber Threat: Koi bhi malicious (bad) activity jo system ko nuksan pahunchaye.

Threat Vectors: Woh raste ya tarike jinse malware ya hacker system me ghus ta hai. Examples: Phishing emails, Infected USB drives, Weak Wi-Fi networks.

2. Malware (Malicious Software)

Malware ek umbrella term hai. Iske types ye hain (Exam me difference pucha ja sakta hai):

  • Virus: Ek file (host) me chipak jata hai. Jab file open karte ho tab active hota hai. (Needs human action).
  • Worm: Standalone hota hai, host ki zaroorat nahi hoti. Network me khud-ba-khud faelta hai (Fast spread).
  • Trojan Horse: Ek useful software ki tarah dikhta hai (e.g., Free Game), par andar se hacking karta hai. (Needs human action).
  • Spyware: Aapki activity chupke se monitor karta hai (Keyloggers - jo password type karte ho wo save karte hain).
  • Ransomware: Aapke files ko encrypt (lock) kar deta hai aur password wapas paane ke liye paise (ransom) maangta hai.
Mnemonic for Malware: Very Wicked Things Spread Rapidly (Virus, Worm, Trojan, Spyware, Ransomware)

3. Advanced Persistent Threats (APTs)

Yeh ek bahut hi khatarnak hacker group hota hai (usually government sponsored). Yeh ek din me attack nahi karte. Yeh system ke andar ghus kar mahino tak chupchap rehte hain aur data churate rehte hain. (Stealthy aur long-term attack).

4. Steganography

Data hiding technique. Isme secret message ko kisi normal file (jaise Image, Audio, Video) ke andar chhupa diya jata hai taaki kisi ko shak na ho. Example: Ek JPG photo ke andar text file chhupa dena.

Mnemonic: Stego = Covered (Chhupana). Cryptography me data dikhta hai par samajh nahi aata, Steganography me data dikhta hi nahi.

5. Credit Card & Debit Card Security

Hone wale Attacks:

  • Skimming: Card reader me chip lagakar card ka data chura lena.
  • Phishing: Fake call/email karke OTP ya CVV maang lena.

Security Measures: EMV Chip (jo card me hota hai), OTP verification, CVV (jo card ke piche hota hai), aur tokenization (card details hide karna online payment me).

  • InfoSec vs IA: InfoSec sirf protect karta hai, IA trust aur non-repudiation guarantee karta hai.
  • Risk Formula: Risk = Threat × Vulnerability × Impact.
  • Virus vs Worm: Virus ko host (file) chahiye, Worm khud faelta hai network me.
  • Trojan: Looks good, acts bad (Fake software).
